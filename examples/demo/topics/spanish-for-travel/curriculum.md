# Spanish for Travel: map (sample)

Built 2026-10-26 for one goal: get through a week in Madrid in March without
switching to English. Spoken practice only, because the test is spoken.

## The modules

### m01. Greetings, politeness and numbers
Covers: greetings, please and thank you, usted against tú, numbers and prices.
Sticking point: prices said digit by digit. You'll know you have this when you
can pay for something quoted at speed.

### m02. Ordering food and drink
Depends on m01. Covers: ordering at a counter and a table, asking about a dish,
the bill. Sticking point: "puedo tener" word for word. You'll know you have this
when you can order a full meal and ask what's in it.

### m03. Asking for and following directions
Depends on m01. Covers: asking for a place, understanding the answer. Sticking
point: derecho against a la derecha. You'll know you have this when you can
follow three spoken turns to a place.

### m04. Hotel check-in and fixing problems
Depends on m01 and m02. Covers: the reservation, the room, something broken.
You'll know you have this when you can get a broken air conditioner fixed.

### m05. Small talk in the past tense
Depends on m02. Covers: what you did today, where you've been.

### m06. Listening at native speed
Depends on m03 and m04. Covers: catching the key words in a fast answer.

## Cut from this map, and why
The subjunctive, formal writing, Latin American vocabulary. None of it shows up
at a Madrid counter in a week.
