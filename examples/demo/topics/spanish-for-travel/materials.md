# Spanish for Travel: materials (sample)

## The goal
Order food, ask directions and check into a hotel in Spanish on a week in
Madrid, without switching to English.

## How you'll be tested
A spoken role-play, about 5 minutes, no notes: a waiter, a hotel clerk and a
stranger asked for directions.

| 2027-03-20 | Flight to Madrid | The deadline |
