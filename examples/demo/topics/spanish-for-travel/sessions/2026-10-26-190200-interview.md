# Interview (sample)

## The real goal
Not "learn Spanish". Get through a week in Madrid in March without switching to
English at a counter: order food, ask directions, check into the hotel and sort
out a problem with the room.

## Where they are now
Two years of high-school Spanish, ten years ago. Reads a menu fine. Freezes when
a waiter speaks at normal speed.

## How they will be tested
A spoken role-play, about 5 minutes, no notes: a waiter, a hotel clerk, a
stranger asked for directions. Practice is spoken, not written.

## What they have already tried
A language app streak that died at day 40. Could match words to pictures, could
not say a sentence when it counted.
